# Haarklous online

A first-person open-world game set around Haarklous plass 17A, Torshov, Oslo: your 4th-floor apartment (your kitchen + bedroom design), its stairwell, the real neighbourhood (OpenStreetMap buildings and streets on Kartverket height data), vehicles, two minigames and a hostage-rescue mission.

**Play online with friends:** open **https://haarklous-online.server-smoke.workers.dev** (or the local file – it connects to the same server). Up to twelve players per lobby.

**Play:** double-click `PLAY-Controller-Windows.cmd` (fullscreen Chrome/Edge window, sound allowed for controller play) or open `haarklous-game.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Everything runs offline; only the optional aerial imagery loads online.

**Minimap** (optional, off at first; tick *Minimap* in the pause menu or press D-pad ↓ there): a round map in the bottom-left corner like GTA IV. It turns with the camera so the way you look is always up (N on the rim shows north), you are the white arrow, places on the map are coloured dots, other players in your lobby are arrows in their colour (on the rim when they are far away), and your waypoint is the purple pin (on the rim when it is out of range). It shows about 90 m around you on foot and zooms out with speed and height (up to 300 m). It hides while the photo camera is out. The setting is saved in the browser. During a disc golf round it is always on (and goes off again afterwards if you had it off) and shows the course: see *Disc golf*.

**Kiwi Storgata and the mushroom wizard** (issue #65): Kiwi Storgata 33 is at its real place in the city centre (🛒 on the map, about 2.8 km south-west of home; open Mon–Sat 07–23 like the real shop), with a counter by the window for a hot coffee and a hot dog. Outside, a few worn-out but kind regulars (☺ on the map) ask for small favours – talk with □ (E); they speak Norwegian with the English below:
- **Kjell-Arne**, a retired sailor on a crate: collect ten empty bottles (□ on bottles along Storgata, or drink a soda) – he gives you his Kvikk Lunsj.
- **Bente**, once in the Opera chorus, with her shopping trolley: fetch her late husband's harmonica from the top of the Opera roof – 100 kr.
- **Tore and his dog Laika**: buy a coffee and a hot dog at the counter inside – a Solo for you.
- **Mona**, a retired postwoman: take her note to her brother Rolf, who feeds the pigeons at Schous plass, and bring his answer – 80 kr.
- **Even**, a retired press photographer: take a good photo of Grønland kirke, the whole tower (the photo camera, △) – 120 kr.
- Each task shows at the top right and sets the waypoint. Each finished task makes Storgata trust you a little more.
- **Sopp-Sigvald**, the mushroom wizard on the corner (🍄), rambles a lot and teaches four powers, one for each mushroom hunt, once enough regulars trust you (1, 2, 3, then 4): star mushrooms in Torshovdalen → **Svev** (levitation), river mushrooms along Akerselva → **Glimt** (blink), the bone mushroom in the catacomb's Rotunda → **Vindkast** (gust), the moon mushroom at MUNCH → **Lyskule** (light orb).
- The powers: R1 (X) casts the chosen one, D-pad ← (←) chooses, the keys 1–4 cast one directly. **Svev**: hold to float up to 3 m (not through ceilings), let go to sink slowly. **Glimt**: up to 8 m the way you face, stopping at walls. **Vindkast**: a gust ahead that pushes the nearest parked car, motorcycle or e-scooter and scares the pigeons (online the others see where it stops). **Lyskule**: a glowing orb by your shoulder that lights up the dark – the catacomb's tunnels too – on and off with the same button. The bar over the powers is your mana; it refills by itself, and each power has a short cooldown. The powers work on foot only – not in a vehicle, the mission, paintball or the timed run. Your tasks and powers are saved with your progress (and your account, when you are logged in); two badges: *Friend of Storgata* and *Mushroom apprentice*.

**Catacombs** (issue #43): three stone mausoleums in Torshovdalen – on the petanque lawn, on the west side of the valley and on the big lawn north of HODET N.N., each about 100 m from the others and marked ☠ on the map – stand over long stairs down to a large catacomb about 15–19 m under the park (about 1.5 km of galleries). Walk in through the open gate and down. Torches burn in the bone galleries (walls of stacked bones with rows of skulls, as in Paris), the rotunda with its bone pillar, the chapels, the crypt and the entrance halls; the old quarry tunnels are dark, and there the prompt offers your torch (□, or L1 / Q any time underground). Down there the map is wiped and draws only what you have seen in the catacombs (kept between visits); the three ways out are always marked. Carved tablets show the streets above, as in Paris.

**Motorcycle (2005 Honda CBF600S) and cars:** □ to get on/in; R2 throttle, L2 brake (hold when stopped to reverse), left stick steers (the bike leans at speed), right stick looks around. Your gloved hands hold the bars or the wheel, which turn with you. The bike has a six-speed gearbox model and an inline-four engine note. Cars and motorcycles follow real gravity (9.81 m/s²): take a crest or bump faster than gravity can hold you on the ground and you fly until you land – no grip, steering or brakes in the air, a thud and a dip when you touch down.

**Helicopter** (Airbus H125 style, on the grass beside Haarklous plass) and **plane** (single-seat sport plane at the north end of the long grass strip in Torshovdalen; both marked on the map as Helipad and Airstrip): □ to get in. They fly with "Mode 2" sticks, the Battlefield default and the usual RC layout:
- Helicopter: left stick ↑↓ collective (centred = hover; lift it a little to get off the ground), ←→ pedals (yaw); right stick pitch and roll (it gently levels itself when you let go). The rotor needs a few seconds to spool up.
- Plane: left stick ↑ adds throttle and ↓ takes it off (it stays where you leave it), ←→ rudder and nose-wheel steering; right stick pitch and roll; L2 wheel brakes. Full throttle, pull back at about 85 km/h, and it lifts off after roughly 80 m.
- Keyboard and mouse (Battlefield style): W/S collective or throttle, A/D pedals or rudder, the mouse pitches and rolls (mouse up = nose down, mouse down = nose up, left/right = roll). How far you move the mouse is how far the aircraft tilts, and it keeps that tilt. Hold Shift (or the right mouse button) to look around; let go and the view turns back. The arrow keys still pitch and roll too.
- R3 (or V) switches between the cockpit and a chase camera. The HUD shows speed, altitude and climb (plus rotor or throttle). Hitting the ground hard or a building fast is a crash: the aircraft is put back on its pad. □ gets out once you have landed and stopped.

**Skydiving** (a Cessna 208 Caravan jump plane at the airstrip in Torshovdalen; on the map as *Skydiving*): three ways up.
- The **jump flight** leaves every 8 minutes on the lobby's shared clock, so everybody sees the same plane: walk to it while it is boarding at the north end of the strip and press □ (the prompt counts down to take-off). It climbs in a spiral to 3000 m, opens the door and gives a 60-second green light: ✕ (Space) jumps. □ gets you off again before take-off; anyone still on board when it flies off is put back at the airstrip.
- Or a player **flies the Caravan** (□ at the cockpit; it flies like the plane, with automatic take-off flaps: full throttle, pull back at about 110 km/h) and the others board at the door behind the wing with □. Jumpers can jump once it is above 300 m. Alone, fly it up yourself and press ✕ (Space) above 300 m: the game's pilot takes the controls and you jump. △ (F) in the cockpit hands it to the game's pilot and sends you to the back.
- Or the **game's pilot** flies the Caravan: board at the door with □ and press △ (F). It waits 10 s for others to board, taxis onto the strip (and waits there if the jump flight is boarding ahead), then flies the same climb to 3000 m and jump run with a green light. With nobody left aboard it flies back to the airstrip. If the Caravan's pilot leaves in the air, the game's pilot takes over. A pilot in a white shirt sits in the left seat while it flies.
- Twelve places on two benches, facing the tail; in a lobby your place is your player number. □ gets you off while the plane stands still.
- **Freefall** (about 54 m/s, 195 km/h, belly down; the real wind drifts you): left stick ↑ / W tracks forward, ↓ / S back-slides, ←→ / A D turn; the right stick or the mouse looks around. A beep at 1500 m and "PULL!" at 1000 m: ✕ (Space) pulls. If you are still in freefall at 300 m, the reserve opens by itself.
- **Canopy** (opens over 3 seconds; about 10 m/s forward and 5 m/s down): ←→ / A D pull the steering toggles, ↑ / W the front risers (faster and steeper), ↓ / S flares – hold it just before the ground. Under 3.5 m/s down is a soft landing, under 6 m/s a hard one; more is a crash (you get up again).
- R3 (or V) switches between your own eyes and a view from behind you. Other players see you fall belly down and then hang under a canopy in your colour.

**FPV drone:** hold △, choose *FPV drone* and release. You see through its camera with an on-screen display (mode, battery, flight time, altitude, throttle, speed, distance home). Left stick ↑↓ thrust (in angle mode it sets the climb rate and holds altitude when centred), ←→ yaw; right stick tilt. With keyboard and mouse it flies like the helicopter: W/S thrust, A/D yaw, the mouse tilts it (mouse up = nose down) and it keeps the tilt; hold Shift (or the right mouse button) to look around. L1 (or Q) switches between *angle* (self-levelling) and *acro* (the right stick sets rotation rates, for flips and rolls, as in real freestyle FPV). It flies indoors too. ✕ resets it after a crash; ○ or △ packs it away. The battery lasts about five minutes.

**Real weather:** the game uses the real weather at Haarklous plass from MET Norway (the data behind yr.no), fetched when the game starts and every hour. Clouds dim the sun and grey the sky, fog thickens the haze, and rain, sleet or snow falls around you outdoors, slanted by the real wind; you hear the wind and the rain. The pause screen shows the temperature, the weather and the wind. Without internet the game keeps its sunny day. Weather data from MET Norway (CC BY 4.0).

**Wristwatch:** D-pad ↑ raises your left wrist. The watch shows the PC's local time (analogue hands and a small digital window) and lowers itself after a few seconds.

**Excavator** (by the street outside the building): □ to climb in. Tracks: L1/L2 left track forward/back, R1/R2 right track forward/back (counter-rotate to turn on the spot). ISO joysticks: left stick left/right swings the house, forward/back pushes the stick (dipper) out/in; right stick forward/back lowers/raises the boom, left/right curls/dumps the bucket. Dig with the teeth in the ground while curling; dump to drop a pile.

**Construction site** (140 × 90 m, fenced, in the south-west part of Torshovdalen, on the map; a park footpath runs in through the north gate and out through the south gate): a graded platform, a 30 × 16 m foundation pit with a ramp, five stockpiles, site offices and seven machines that drive like the real ones (engine power, grip, gears, rolling resistance on soil, slopes, hydraulics) and soil you can really move – buckets and blades cut it, carry it, pour it into a truck or onto the ground, and loose soil slides to its natural slope (nothing is lost). □ to climb in; on a keyboard the arrow keys work as the right stick.
- Dozer (Cat D6 class): R2 forward, L2 reverse, left stick steer; right stick back/forward raises/lowers the blade, ←→ tilts it. Drive into a heap with the blade low to push soil.
- Excavator (30 t): L1/L2 left track, R1/R2 right track; left stick swing and stick, right stick boom and bucket (the same ISO pattern as the street excavator). Teeth in the soil while curling digs; turn the bucket over a truck to load it.
- Wheel loader: R2 throttle, L2 brake/reverse, left stick steers (articulated); right stick back/forward lifts/lowers, ← rolls the bucket back, → dumps. Drive into a stockpile with the bucket low to scoop.
- Articulated hauler and tipper truck: R2 / L2 / left stick drive; right stick back tips the body, forward lowers it (with the body up they only creep).
- Mobile crane: drive it like a truck; stopped, L1 lowers the outriggers (L1 again raises them, folding the boom first). On the outriggers: left stick ←→ slew, ↑↓ telescope out/in, right stick back/forward boom up/down, R2 / L2 hoist up/down, ✕ hooks the load under the hook or sets it down.
- Tower crane (climb in at the foot of the mast): left stick ←→ slew (it has inertia), ↑↓ trolley out/in; right stick back/forward hoists up/down; ✕ hooks or sets down a load (brick pallets, steel beams, a concrete skip).
R3 (keyboard V) switches the view of a machine: from the cab, from outside (the mouse turns it round the machine) and, for the cranes, straight down from above the hook. The HUD shows speed and gear, the bucket or body load in m³, the blade height, or the crane's boom, radius and hook.

**Paintball** (an urban course on the valley floor of Torshovdalen, between the sculpture and the construction site, 60 × 40 m inside a net: containers, roofless buildings, walls with windows, barriers, a car wreck, cable spools, sandbags, barrels, pallets and tyres – the same for both teams). Walk into the lobby on the lawn outside the gate: the first one there is the admin; others choose a team with ↑↓ (any, blue or red) and wait. The admin picks the game with ←→ (team deathmatch with respawns, team deathmatch in rounds, or capture the flag), paint or reball with △, and holds ✕ to start a game for everyone in the lobby (up to 6 v 6; wishes first, the rest balanced). Normal paintball rules: a ball that breaks on you (or your marker) puts you out, also from your own team; a bounce does not count (reball: every hit counts). Semi-automatic marker, 10 balls a second at most, 200 in the hopper. R2 (left mouse) fires, L2 (right mouse) aims, □ (R) reloads, hold △ for 2 s to leave the game. Team deathmatch: first to 20 eliminations (back in at your base after 5 s) or, in rounds, out is out until one team is left, first to 3 rounds. Capture the flag: bring their flag to your base, first to 3 captures; a carrier who is hit drops it. Games last at most 10 minutes; paint stays on the cover until the next game. Alone you can practise; online everyone in the lobby plays together. In a game you only see your team-mates' name tags; your opponents' names are hidden.

**HODET N.N.** (Marianne Heske, 2014): the 7 m bronze doll's head stands at its real place at the north end of Torshovdalen, facing south down the valley, with stone steps on its east side that you can climb and sit on. It is on the map.

**Torshovoksen** (the bull on Oksefontenen, Asbjørg Borgfelt, 1929): the bronze bull charging with its head low stands at its real place on the fountain in the middle of Hegermanns plass, facing south-south-west along Mastrups gate, with its two basins and four water-spouting lizards. It is on the map. The model is a simple likeness.

**Disc golf**: two courses. **Muselunden DiscGolfPark**, the real 12-hole course at its real place by Sinsenkrysset, about 700 m north-east of home (layout "Muselunden 12 hull (2022)", all par 3, 35–106 m; on the map), and two par-3 holes of about 70 m in Torshovdalen. Stand on a tee pad, □ to play. Aim with the camera, hold R2 for power and release to throw; left stick ←/→ adds hyzer/anhyzer. Within 11 m you putt. Chains catch slow discs; scorecard on screen. After each hole you walk (or run) to the next tee, and the hole starts when you get there; □ takes you straight there. ○ quits.

During a round the minimap shows the course: its outline, every hole's tee (with its number), basket and a line from tee to basket. The hole you play is bright, the others faded. Your lie (where your last throw landed) is a pink dot with the metres to the basket. The minimap zooms so the whole hole fits, and between holes it shows the way to the next tee (a dashed line). The big map (touchpad) shows the same layout while a round is on.

**Petanque** (gravel court in the park): □ to play singles against Arne (3 boules each, first to 13). The jack must land 6–10 m away; the player not holding the point throws next; at the end of each mène the leader scores one point per boule closer than the opponent's best; the next circle is where the jack ended. Hold R2 for distance and release; hold L2 to shoot (flat throw that knocks boules). Throws land roughly where you aim, with scatter. The HUD shows the score, mène, boules left, who holds the point and by how much, and distance labels over the boules.

**Home:** the street door of 17A is an ordinary door – go in, up the stairs to the 4th floor and through your front door, no mission needed. **Hostage rescue:** stand in the red ring with the sign beside the entrance and press □. After a mission (finished or not), choosing *Leave* keeps you where you are – if you are in the apartment, you simply stay at home. A hostage is in the 4th-floor apartment; 3–5 armed hostiles are in the apartment and in the stairwell. The pistol, health bar and hostile/hostage counter exist only during the mission. L2 aims, R2 fires, □ opens the door in front or reloads, Options pauses.

**Stashes:** three hidden crates – on the top landing of a neighbouring stairwell, in the courtyard behind the building, and by the bushes in the park north of the block. Each has lines to snort and a syringe: □ gives a random 30-second boost – 3× running speed, 3× jump height or bullet time (the world, including the mission's hostiles, slows to about a third while you keep full speed). A used item is back after 90 seconds.

**Shops and money:** Joker Torshov (about 120 m south-west of the flat) and Kiwi Torshov (about 250 m north) are at their real places from OpenStreetMap, built into the ground floor of their buildings, with a glass front, sliding doors, shelves, fridges, firewood, a bottle-return machine and a cashier at the till (both on the map, 🛒). The opening hours are on the door but the shops are always open in the game. Walk in, look at an item and press □ to put it in your basket, then pay at the till with □. You start with 500 kr; the top-right corner shows your money and empty bottles, and the basket shows what you are carrying. Kiwi is about 5 % cheaper. Norwegian rules: beer is only sold Monday–Friday 08–20 and Saturday 08–18 (PC clock), never on Sunday – the cashier takes the beer back. Without enough money the card is declined. Walking out with unpaid items sets off the door alarm and they go back. Bought items go in your bag (D-pad ↓): drinks give an empty bottle that the machine takes for 2 kr, food sobers you up, Red Bull gives 15 s of 3× speed, a beer is +30 s drunk (the beers in the flat and the firepit crate leave an empty bottle too), and a sack of firewood can be emptied onto the firepit (+3 logs). Earn money: hostage rescue 400 kr, disc golf par 50 / birdie 100 / eagle 200 kr, petanque win 150 kr, bottle deposit 2 kr. Money, bottles and bag are saved in the browser.

**Drinks:** six beers on the kitchen worktop and a bottle of aquavit on the TV bench. Each beer or sip adds 30 s of drunkenness: blurred, doubled, swaying vision and drifting walking/steering.

**Soundtrack** (generated live, no sound files, loops seamlessly; volume in the pause menu):

- Disc golf: *Fairway Breeze*, breezy acoustic guitar picking with shaker and a whistled tune (1:20).

**Jobs (delivery and taxi):** open the phone (D-pad → / → or P) or press □ at a *JOBB* sign beside Joker and Kiwi. *Deliver food*: an order comes in from a real café, restaurant or takeaway near Torshov (OpenStreetMap) or from Joker / Kiwi; the waypoint and the minimap show the way and a yellow ring marks the spot. □ there picks up the order, and then you have a time limit to take it to a real street door (an OpenStreetMap address; a door with its house number appears on the building). □ at the door hands it over: 35 kr + 7 kr per 100 m, up to 50 % more for being fast, only 40 % when late. Walk, run, ride a bike or scooter, or drive. Orders keep coming until you stop in the phone. *Taxi*: sit in a car as the driver and turn on taxi in the phone (the roof light comes on). A passenger calls from a street address; stop beside them and they get in the back and ask for an address in the neighbourhood. The fare is 65 kr + 20 kr per km along the streets; smooth and fast driving gives a tip of up to a quarter of the fare, while crashes, hard braking and swerving, wrong turns and drunk driving make the passenger angry (the bar on the job card) and the tip smaller or nothing. Getting out ends the taxi shift (a passenger in the back leaves without paying). Online the others see your taxi light and your passenger, and a friend who rides along (passenger seat or pillion) sees your job and gets half of each payment.

**E-scooters and city bikes:** e-scooters stand on the pavements around Torshov and Grünerløkka. □ unlocks one for 10 kr, then 3 kr every started minute (a typical Oslo price); you ride standing, alone, at up to 20 km/h (the Norwegian limit). Getting off ends the ride and the scooter stays where you left it – for the other players in your lobby too. The Oslo city bikes stand in the real Oslo Bysykkel docks around Torshov, Myrens and Grünerløkka (40 docks, from the operator's open data; each shows up to 10 of its slots). On the map every dock is a blue dot with the number of free bikes; zoomed in it shows the name and how many are bikes and e-bikes. □ takes a bike or an e-bike from the dock: 8 kr, then 1 kr (e-bike 2 kr) for every started minute after the first – the real prices. Pedal with R2: faster than walking, slower than a motorcycle and harder uphill; the e-bike's motor helps you up to 25 km/h. Stop at any dock with a free slot and □ returns the bike (the trip ends and is paid). □ anywhere else locks the bike and the trip goes on – come back and □ to ride on, but if it is not in a dock within 15 minutes you pay a 250 kr fee for not returning it (the game's own rule) and the bike stays where it is. Nobody else can take a bike you have locked. Without enough money you cannot unlock.

**Multiplayer:** every player has an own 4-character lobby code (pause menu, with a share link `…/?lobby=CODE`). Friends join with the code (pause menu: type it and *Join lobby*, or on the controller press △ and pick the letters with the D-pad, ✕ to join; □ takes you back to your own lobby). Up to twelve players. You see each other (name tags, jacket colour per player), doors and the firepit are shared, and vehicles driven by someone move for everyone. Next to a vehicle someone else is driving, □ rides along as passenger (motorcycle pillion, cars, helicopter, plane – one passenger each). **Chat:** Enter or T on the keyboard; the controller's Share button opens quick chat (choose with ↑↓, ✕ sends). Chat lines also appear as bubbles above the speaker. **Voice chat:** tick *Voice chat* in the pause menu – everyone in the lobby hears you at full volume, wherever they are on the map (you hear the others even with your own microphone off).

**Firepit** (in the park in front of the building, on the map): □ by a bench to sit down (R2 for a beer from the crate, □ to stand up), □ at the woodpile throws a log on the fire – it gets bigger, brighter and louder, and burns down again over a few minutes. More cars (30) and motorcycles (8) are parked around the neighbourhood.

**Badges and the photo hunt** (issue #58): the pause menu's *Badges and best scores* (R1 / B) lists 18 badges from playing – finish the hostage mission, a hole in one, a full round at Muselunden, beat Arne at petanque, win a paintball game against other players, land within 10 m of the orange skydiving target 180 m down the airstrip (◎ on the map), climb a route and pass the brattkort at Klatreverket, kayak down Akerselva, beat Ola at table tennis, finish the Ensjø → Rathkes gate run, go down into the catacomb, open Moby-Dick, throw a log on the firepit, fly 50 m up, help all five regulars at Kiwi Storgata, learn all four powers from Sopp-Sigvald – and a **photo hunt** of ten places: Torshovoksen, HODET N.N., Deichman Grünerløkka, Klatreverket Torshov, the Rotunda of bones in the catacomb, Muselunden (the basket of hole 1), MUNCH, the Opera, Rathkes gate 1 and your own street door. A photo counts when the place is in the picture (not at the very edge), big enough (go closer, or zoom in with L2 and the right stick) and nothing solid is in the way; the street door counts only from the front. The list shows your photo, or a hint for the places you have not found. Each new badge shows a message and pays 50–300 kr. The same list keeps your best scores: disc golf rounds per course, holes in one, petanque and table tennis wins, paintball wins, the mission's best time, skydiving jumps and the closest landing, the kayak and run times and Klatreverket.

**Your progress with an account** (issue #54): without an account everything stays in this browser, as before. Logged in, your money and bottles, the bag, your photos, badges, best scores, Klatreverket ticks, the catacomb map and your settings (look sensitivity, music volume, minimap) are your account's: they load when you log in on any PC or browser and save by themselves every 20 s and when the tab is hidden or closed. The first time you log in on a browser that already has progress, the game asks whether to move it into the account (money, bottles and the bag add up, the better best scores win, badges and photos join). Say no and it stays in this browser and comes back when you log out. Photos go to the account's own album, at most 60 – delete old ones in the gallery. The graphics settings stay per PC.

**Exploring:** walk the whole neighbourhood (about 1.5 km square round home). Eight neighbouring buildings have a working stair tower you can walk up; their flat doors are locked.

**Klatreverket Torshov** (issue #40): the climbing centre at its real place, Myrens verksted 3K by Akerselva, about 1 km west-south-west of the flat (on the map, 🧗; walk there over the Myrens ground). It is in its real building – the hall wing is 60 × 20 m and, from Kartverket's surface model, 14.5 m high to the eaves, so the walls are 14 m like the real ones.
- **Inside, like the real gym:** *Hallen* with 14 m walls of different steepness (slab, vertical, overhangs, a prow) and top-ropes on about half the routes; *Konkurranseveggen*, 14 m high and 7 m overhanging, lead only; *Galleriet* and *Kursrommet* with 10 m walls and a top-rope on every route; the autobelay tower with six 14 m lines (orange stripes, yellow safety gates). Bouldering on three floors: *Hula* (a roof cave with the easier grades) on the ground floor, *Discorommet* (low 3.5 m walls, the slab corner, a disco ball) on the 2nd floor, the inner room with steeper walls and harder problems on the 3rd; the training room on the 4th floor with the Moonwall (40°, problems lit by LEDs from the control box), the Vippevegg, a system wall, the technique corner, a campus ladder and a hangboard. Steel stairs connect the floors; the upper floors look out over the hall.
- **The reception** (□ at the counter): day ticket 155 kr before 16 on weekdays, 175 kr after 16 and at weekends (PC clock); rent climbing shoes 50, harness 50, chalk 20, or all three for 100 kr; the free autobelay introduction; the brattkort test (top rope, 150 / 100 kr) when the climbing staff is on duty – after 17 on weekdays, after 11 at weekends. Klatresjappa, the price board and the opening hours are there too (the gym is always open in the game, like the shops).
- **Climbing:** look at a hold of a route and press □ (a card at the bottom of each line shows its colour, grade and style). Hold L2 + R2 (both mouse buttons, or Q + R) to grip; let go with one hand, look at the next hold and grip again to reach it – a green ring means in reach, orange means you must jump (✕), red is too far. Your feet find footholds by themselves. The left stick moves your body; push it up to stand tall and reach further. The pump meter fills faster on steep walls, small holds and one hand; two big holds let you shake out, □ chalks up. Bouldering: match both hands on the TOP hold, ○ drops you onto the mat. Top rope: Siri (your climbing partner, she has a brattkort) belays you; ○ takes, ○ again lowers you. Lead: □ clips the next quickdraw and then the anchor – a fall is twice as far as you are above your last clip, and with nothing clipped you hit the ground. Autobelay: let go and it lowers you. Routes use the Norwegian grades (3 to 8+), boulders the Font grades (3 to 7B); your sends are saved (flash / sent / top rope / autobelay).
- **Belaying:** pass the brattkort test (three questions, then belay Siri: R2 takes in the rope as she climbs, L2 is the brake when she falls, hold ✕ to lower her) and you can belay Siri whenever you like (□ when you look at her).
- Other players in your lobby see you climbing and hanging on the rope.

**Gamle Oslo, Ensjø and the way there** (issue #38): south of the old neighbourhood the world goes on for about 3.4 × 3.4 km – down Trondheimsveien past Carl Berner and Sofienberg to Schous plass, and through Rodeløkka, Tøyen, Kampen, Vålerenga, Ensjø, Grønland, Gamlebyen and Bjørvika to the fjord. Real streets, real ground heights and about 7,900 buildings in their real places (simple shapes, not exact copies), parks with their mapped trees, and the aerial photo on the ground when online (it loads when you come within about 700 m). Key buildings are modelled: Sofienberg, Paulus, Kampen, Vålerenga, Grønland, Gamlebyen and Tøyen churches with their towers and clocks, MUNCH with its bent top, the Opera – walk up its white roof from the plaza – and the tall brick chimneys such as Freiapipa. You stop at the quay; the water is not walkable. Neighbourhood names are on the map, and your photos are named after the nearest one.

**Ensjø → Rathkes gate run:** a green ring outside Ensjø T-bane (on the map, ▶) starts a timed run: stand in it and press □. The clock stops in the chequered ring in Rathkes gate by Sofienbergparken (⚑), about 1.5 km away as the crow flies and 55 m lower. Any way counts – run, ride or drive. Your best time is kept in the browser; a finish pays 30 kr, a new best 60 kr. At the finish, □ takes you back to Ensjø for another try.

**Akerselva by kayak** (issue #45): three kayak racks on the river bank – Bjølsen (in the old neighbourhood, 4.7 km to go), Sagene by Sagveien (2.6 km) and Grünerløkka by Torggata (0.9 km), on the map (🛶). □ at a rack puts you in a kayak. Left stick ↑ / W paddles forward, ↓ / S back-paddles, ←→ / A D turn; the right stick or the mouse looks around; R3 / V switches to a view from behind. The river is the real one: its course, width and water level from OpenStreetMap and Kartverket, 81 m of drop with ten falls and rapids (white water, a splash and a shake), the current faster where it drops, 46 bridges to paddle under. The run ends at Vaterland, just before the river goes into the culvert under the city (a boom stops you); your best time from each rack is kept, a finish pays 40 kr, a new best 80 kr. □ gets you out on the nearer bank when you are slow and close to it.

**Schous plass and Deichman Grünerløkka** (issues #44 and #47): an outdoor concrete table-tennis table in the park at Schous plass – □ to play Ola, first to 11 (two serves each, one each from 10–10, win by two). Walk quietly: if you run inside, or talk loudly on voice chat there, the librarian (glasses, curly hair, scarf) comes at you, shushes you and walks back to her desk – and you walk for a while.

**Rathkes gate 1** (issue #44, preparation): the way to a second flat, Rathkes gate 1C on the 5th floor, is ready – the iron gate under the "1" on Rathkes gate (□ opens it), the passage under the building and the backyard with the doors 1B and 1C. ## Development

Edit the source files, then `python build.py` to rebuild `haarklous-game.html`. `python generate-surroundings.py` regenerates `surroundings-data.js` from the OSM and DTM files (about two minutes). District patches beyond the old square: `python tools/fetch-district.py [name x0 s0 x1 s1]` downloads OpenStreetMap and Kartverket data for a box (local metres, multiples of 16) and `python tools/make-district.py [name ...]` writes `<name>-data.js` (default: `gamle-oslo`); add a new patch's script tag after `gamle-oslo-data.js` in `index.html`. `node --test tests/*.cjs` runs the tests; `node tests/online-paintball.mjs` plays a full 12-player paintball lobby against a local lobby server (`npx wrangler dev --port 8787` in `cloud/`, never the live one); `tests/browser-harness.js` drives the game in a browser with a simulated controller. `python package-portable.py` writes `dist/Haarklous-online.zip`.

**Server:** `cloud/` is a Cloudflare Worker (account 46fd597da1c5fe9de4c3ebbaae86f906) that serves the game (`cloud/public/index.html`, written by `build.py`) and runs one Durable Object per lobby (`cloud/src/index.js`). After `python build.py`, deploy from `cloud/` with `npx wrangler deploy` (Wrangler is logged in on this PC). For local testing: `npx wrangler dev --port 8787` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8787/?server=ws://127.0.0.1:8787`.

**Saved progress (issue #54):** `game-progress.js` (which localStorage keys are progress, how two copies join, the sync with the server) and `cloud/src/progress.js` (one Durable Object `Profile` per account with SQLite storage: `/api/progress`, `/api/photos/*`, only for the logged-in player). For a local test without the database: `npx wrangler dev --var DEV_USER:tester` in `cloud/` makes every request on localhost logged in as `tester`. Badges and the photo hunt: `game-badges.js`.

**Accounts (optional, pause menu):** email and password through Better Auth in the Worker (`cloud/src/auth.js`, routes `/api/auth/*`), stored in the Supabase project "Haarklous Online" (ref `sxhqirmecalxcnyojjki`, schema `game_auth`, made by `cloud/sql/accounts.sql`). The Worker reaches Supabase through Cloudflare Hyperdrive `haarklous-db` as the role `haarklous_worker`. One-time setup and new database password: run `npm install` and then `node scripts/set-db-secret.mjs` in `cloud/` (needs `npx supabase login` and Wrangler logged in), then `npx wrangler deploy`. Accounts work only on the Worker's own host; `wrangler dev` without a local Postgres serves the game but cannot log in.
