HAARKLOUS PLASS 17A · TORSHOV · OSLO

Haarklous online

Walk the neighbourhood, go home to your 4th-floor apartment (street door of 17A, up the stairs), go into the stairwells, ride the Honda CBF600, drive the parked cars or the excavator, work the machines on the construction site, go skydiving from the jump plane, go bouldering and rope climbing at Klatreverket Torshov, play paintball, disc golf in Torshovdalen and petanque in the park, take photos, earn money delivering food or driving a taxi (phone: D-pad → / P), ride e-scooters and Oslo city bikes from their real docks, and start the hostage-rescue mission at the red marker beside the entrance of number 17A. Further south the world goes on down Trondheimsveien to Gamle Oslo, Ensjø and Bjørvika – real streets, ground heights and buildings, the churches, MUNCH and the Opera's walkable roof – with a timed run from Ensjø T-bane to Rathkes gate (green ring, □). In Grünerløkka: paddle a kayak down Akerselva from Bjølsen, Sagene or Grünerløkka to Vaterland, play table tennis at Schous plass, browse Deichman Grünerløkka (walk – the librarian hears everything) and go through the gate of Rathkes gate 1 to its backyard. Play together: up to twelve players per lobby – your lobby code is in the pause menu, and there you can also log in or create an account (optional, email and password).
