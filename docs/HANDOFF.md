### Disc golf course on the minimap (issue #60, PR #61, on `main` and live since 3 Nov 2026)
- `game-minigames.js` disc golf: after a hole `pending.next` sets `game.walk` = the next hole's index and `update` returns `{walk: true}` (no teleport). game-main's `updateMinigame` then runs `updateFoot` (□ and ○ are taken out of `input.pressed` first; look and the R2 charge are skipped). `input.at` = your feet; within 3 m (and 3 m in height) of the tee, or with `input.skip` (□), `beginHole(i)` returns the teleport onto the tee facing the basket. `g.r2Wait` stops R2 held from running from charging a throw until it is let go. `game.landed` = a throw on this hole has come to rest (the lie dot shows only then).
- `game.layout()` (null when no round): `{name, outline (MUSELUNDEN.outline or null), holes [{n, tee, basket}] (local [x, s]), current, walking, lie [x, s] | null, distance}`. `GameMinigames.create` now takes `toLocal` (game-main passes `world.toLocal`).
- `game-map.js`: game-main's `updateGuide` sets `map.course` every frame (null outside a round). `drawCourse(g, c, P, d, z, you, rim)` draws on the minimap (screen space through `at()`, clipped by the circle, size 1) and the big map (size 1.4). A null outline becomes `courseOutline(holes, 14)` (convex hull of 12-point circles round every tee and basket, cached per course name). The minimap range is `courseRange(you, points)` (furthest point × 1.12 + 8 m, 40–300 m) instead of `minimapRange`, and the minimap shows while `map.course` is set whatever the saved setting. Map icons with `course` equal to the round's name are hidden during it.
