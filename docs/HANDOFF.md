### Ground and building textures (issues #67 and #68, PR #81, branch `feat/better-textures`, 4 Nov 2026)
- **`city-textures.js`** (new, `window.CityTextures`, Node-tested pure parts): `zoneAt(x, s)` (rough boxes: torshov, sandaker, myrens, lokka, toyen, gronland, kampen, ensjo, bjorvika), `classify(info)` → look `{cls, upper, ground, plinth, top, roof, tint, roofTint, plinthH, groundH, topH, shops}`, `wallBands(look, g0, bottom, top, shop)` (plinth / ground floor / whole storeys / cornice; mode 0 repeats up the wall, 1 is a band; bands touch, never overlap), `shopLook`, `edgeU` (whole bays per edge, short edges show only the plain pier at u < .12), `looksOf(BUILDING_LOOKS)`. `create(T, renderer)` is made once per page (lib.facade shares it).
- **Facade atlas:** 2048 × 1024 RGBA `DataTexture` (sRGB, mipmaps, anisotropy), 32 cells of 256 px with 8 px wrap-around borders (`CELL_LIST`: name, bay width m, storey height m). Alpha is a mask: 1 = takes the building's tint (`aTint`), .5 = keeps its own colour (brick, frames, sills, stone), 0 = glass. Half-size normal atlas from a painted height layer. Cells are painted on 240 px CPU canvases and copied into typed arrays (the big atlas canvas readback took about a second). Vertex attributes: `aCell` = (cell index, mode, building seed), `aTint`. Shader (`material()`): atlas UV from fract(uv) inside the cell, `textureGrad` with the LOD capped at 3 (border size), the cell's average colour (`uAvg0 + uAvg1 × tint`) from LOD 3 to 4.5 instead of shimmer, per-window curtains / blinds / a lit room from a hash of the bay and storey, glass roughness .18. Roofs use the same atlas (cells 24–27, UV = metres / tile). One mesh per near tile (270 m old square, 400 m district tiles); far buildings are one vertex-coloured mesh (`farColour(cell, tint)`).
- **Looks data:** `tools/make-building-looks.cjs` reads `assets/osm/torshov-osm.json` (old square: matched to `D.buildings` by the outline holding the centre) and `assets/osm/building-looks-osm-2026-11-03.json.gz` (Overpass: tags of all 11,903 buildings in the game area and 2,072 ground-floor shop / café nodes; `--fetch` gets it again) → `building-looks-data.js` (57 KB): `table` of distinct tag rows, `old` (2 base-36 chars per old building), `ids` + `codes` (district buildings by OSM id; plain blocks of flats left out), `shops` (local x, s). OSM has few look tags here: building:colour 79, roof:colour 96, roof:shape 1,216, heritage 261, start_date 33, building:material 15 – the rest comes from type, height and the part of town. Torshov's building:colour on brick blocks is reddish (#cb9c89), on plastered ones yellow / pink; `brickish()` tells them apart.
- **Who keeps what:** buildings with `b.enter` (stair towers) and the Joker / Kiwi buildings (`GameShops.SHOPS[].building`) are forced to `torshovBrick` with brown frames and no painted shop front, because `lib.facade()` (own building, shop fills) is the atlas's `brick` cell (240 px canvas, 3 × 2.85 m) and `lib.plinth()` its `plinthConcrete` cell. `lib.brick()`'s default palette is darker (Torshov's red-brown).

- **Ground (`ground(mat)`):** the near (4096²), outer (2048²) and district-near terrain materials get one shared 1024² detail texture (R grass blades, G asphalt grain / cracks / patches, B granite slabs 60 × 45 cm, A gravel and soil; 1.8 m tile) sampled at two scales (the second rotated, 5.9 m) plus value noise at 2.9, 11, 43 and 166 m. ### Helicopter and drone lift (issue #73, PR #75, on `main` and live since 3 Nov 2026)
- `game-aircraft.js` Helicopter `update`: rotor thrust `lift` along the rotor axis `u` = rpm² · G · (1 + 0.4 · coll) up (1 + 0.8 · coll down) · ground effect; vertical part cos(tilt), the rest pushes along. Heave damping 0.45 · v.y (world y, kept) and drag v · (0.001 · speed + 0.04). Full collective level climbs about 8 m/s at equilibrium; level flight at full collective needs cos(tilt) = 1 / 1.4 (44°) and tops out near 285 km/h. No translational lift (the issue asks that pitching forward climbs less).
- Drone angle mode: `thrust = G + (−ly · 7 − v.y) · 2`, no division by cos(pitch) · cos(roll) any more, so a tilt sinks it (steady state v.y ≈ −G (1/cos − 1) / (2 cos + 0.18)). Acro unchanged (direct thrust).
